How to play

  1. Clear the grid without clicking on any mines.
  2. Begin by clicking on a cell. If it’s a mine, game over. If not, the cell will show a number or clear nearby cells.
  3. Each number on a cell tells you how many mines are in the 8 neighboring cells around it. Use these numbers as clues to avoid mines.
  4. Right-click on cells you suspect have mines to mark them with a flag. This helps you keep track, but use flags carefully!
  5. The timer starts as soon as you click the first cell and tracks how long you take to complete the game. Aim to clear the grid as quickly as possible to set a high score, especially on higher levels.
  6. You win by revealing all non-mine cells without hitting any mines!
